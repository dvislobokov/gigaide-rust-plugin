Rust postfix template: type the key after an expression and press Tab. The expression is what ends right before the dot: a name, a path, a literal, a call, an indexing or a chain of fields and methods (&x.ref wraps x). rust-analyzer offers type-aware postfix completions of its own in the completion list.
